# Building Better Habits

Habits shape our lives more than we realize.

## The Habit Loop

Every habit consists of:
1. **Cue**: The trigger
2. **Routine**: The behavior
3. **Reward**: The benefit

## How to Build Good Habits

### Start Small

- Begin with tiny actions
- Build momentum gradually
- Celebrate small wins

### Stack Habits

Connect new habits to existing ones:
- After I brush my teeth, I will meditate
- After I pour my coffee, I will journal

### Design Your Environment

- Make good habits obvious
- Remove friction for desired behaviors
- Add friction for bad habits

## Breaking Bad Habits

- Identify triggers
- Replace rather than remove
- Be patient with yourself

## Key Takeaways

- Consistency beats intensity
- Focus on identity, not outcomes
- Progress, not perfection

Start with one habit and master it before adding more!
