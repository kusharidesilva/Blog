# Healthy Eating Habits

Good nutrition is the foundation of a healthy lifestyle.

## Key Principles

### Eat Whole Foods

Focus on foods in their natural state:
- Fruits and vegetables
- Whole grains
- Lean proteins
- Healthy fats

### Stay Hydrated

- Aim for 8 glasses of water daily
- Limit sugary drinks
- Herbal teas are great alternatives

### Practice Portion Control

- Use smaller plates
- Eat slowly and mindfully
- Listen to hunger cues

## Meal Planning Tips

1. Plan meals for the week
2. Prep ingredients in advance
3. Keep healthy snacks available
4. Cook at home more often

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Skipping breakfast
- Eating too fast
- Emotional eating
- Restrictive dieting

Remember: Small, sustainable changes lead to lasting results!
