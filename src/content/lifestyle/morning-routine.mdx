# Morning Routine for Success

How you start your morning sets the tone for your entire day.

## The Power of Mornings

Successful people often credit their morning routines for their productivity.

## Ideal Morning Routine

### 1. Wake Up Early

Give yourself time before the world demands your attention.

### 2. Hydrate

Start with a glass of water to rehydrate your body.

### 3. Move Your Body

Even 10 minutes of exercise can energize you:
- Stretching
- Yoga
- Quick workout

### 4. Mindfulness

Take time for meditation or journaling.

### 5. Healthy Breakfast

Fuel your body with nutritious food.

### 6. Plan Your Day

Review your goals and priorities.

## Tips for Building the Habit

- Start small and build gradually
- Prepare the night before
- Be consistent, even on weekends
- Track your progress

Remember: The best routine is one you'll actually follow!
