# Productivity Hacks for 2026

Work smarter, not harder with these productivity tips.

## Digital Tools

### Task Management

- Notion for all-in-one workspace
- Todoist for simple task lists
- Linear for project management

### Time Tracking

- RescueTime for automatic tracking
- Toggl for manual tracking
- Understand where your time goes

### Focus Tools

- Website blockers
- Focus mode on devices
- Noise-canceling headphones

## Techniques

### Time Blocking

Schedule specific tasks in your calendar.

### The 2-Minute Rule

If it takes less than 2 minutes, do it now.

### Deep Work Sessions

- 90-minute focused blocks
- No interruptions
- Single tasking only

## Environment

- Clean workspace
- Good lighting
- Plants for mood
- Minimize visual clutter

## Habits

- Morning routine
- Regular breaks
- Exercise
- Adequate sleep

Productivity is personal—find what works for you!
