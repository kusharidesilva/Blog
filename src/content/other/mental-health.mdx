# Mental Health Awareness

Mental health is just as important as physical health.

## Understanding Mental Health

Mental health affects how we:
- Think
- Feel
- Act
- Handle stress
- Relate to others

## Common Challenges

### Anxiety

- Excessive worry
- Physical symptoms
- Avoidance behaviors

### Depression

- Persistent sadness
- Loss of interest
- Changes in sleep/appetite

### Burnout

- Exhaustion
- Cynicism
- Reduced effectiveness

## Self-Care Strategies

### Daily Practices

- Exercise regularly
- Sleep adequately
- Eat nutritiously
- Connect with others

### Mindfulness

- Meditation
- Deep breathing
- Present moment awareness

### Boundaries

- Learn to say no
- Limit social media
- Protect your energy

## When to Seek Help

It's okay to ask for help. Consider professional support if:
- Symptoms persist
- Daily life is affected
- You're struggling to cope

## Resources

- National hotlines
- Therapy apps
- Support groups
- Trusted friends and family

You are not alone. Your mental health matters.
