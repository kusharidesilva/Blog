# Financial Planning Basics

Build a strong financial foundation.

## Budgeting

### The 50/30/20 Rule

- 50% Needs
- 30% Wants
- 20% Savings

### Track Your Spending

- Use apps
- Review monthly
- Identify patterns

## Emergency Fund

### Why You Need One

- Job loss protection
- Unexpected expenses
- Peace of mind

### How Much

- Start with $1,000
- Build to 3-6 months expenses

## Debt Management

### Strategies

**Snowball Method**: Pay smallest debts first
**Avalanche Method**: Pay highest interest first

### Avoid New Debt

- Live below your means
- Use credit wisely

## Investing

### Start Early

Compound interest is powerful.

### Diversify

- Stocks
- Bonds
- Real estate
- Index funds

### Retirement Accounts

- 401(k) with employer match
- IRA options

## Key Principles

- Pay yourself first
- Avoid lifestyle inflation
- Stay consistent
- Keep learning

Financial freedom is a journey, not a destination.
