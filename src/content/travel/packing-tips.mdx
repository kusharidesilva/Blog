# Packing Like a Pro

Master the art of packing and travel lighter.

## The Golden Rule

Pack half of what you think you need.

## Choosing Luggage

### Carry-On Only

- Faster travel
- No lost bags
- More mobility
- Cost savings

### When to Check

- Extended trips
- Special equipment
- Winter destinations

## Packing Method

### Rolling vs Folding

- Roll casual clothes
- Fold dress clothes
- Use packing cubes

### The Capsule Wardrobe

- Neutral base colors
- Mix and match pieces
- Layer for weather
- One nice outfit

## Essential Items

### Always Pack

- Passport/ID
- Phone and charger
- Medications
- Basic toiletries

### Often Forgotten

- Adapter/converter
- Sunscreen
- Reusable water bottle
- Snacks

## Packing List by Trip Type

### Beach

- Swimwear
- Cover-up
- Sandals
- Hat and sunglasses

### City

- Comfortable walking shoes
- Smart casual clothes
- Day bag

### Adventure

- Quick-dry clothing
- Sturdy footwear
- First aid kit

Less is more—you can always buy what you need!
