# Travel Photography Tips

Make your travel memories last forever with better photos.

## Gear Essentials

### Camera Options

- Smartphone (convenient)
- Mirrorless (quality + portable)
- DSLR (professional)

### Accessories

- Extra batteries
- Memory cards
- Portable tripod
- Lens cleaning kit

## Composition Tips

### Rule of Thirds

Divide frame into 9 sections, place subjects on lines.

### Leading Lines

Use roads, rivers, or architecture to guide the eye.

### Framing

Use natural frames like doorways or branches.

## Lighting

### Golden Hour

Best light at sunrise and sunset.

### Blue Hour

Magical light just before sunrise or after sunset.

### Avoid Harsh Midday

Or use shadows creatively.

## Capturing Moments

### Tell a Story

- Details and wide shots
- People and places
- Food and culture

### Be Patient

Wait for the right moment.

### Respect Privacy

Always ask before photographing people.

## Post-Processing

- Don't over-edit
- Maintain natural colors
- Back up your photos

Practice makes perfect—keep shooting!
