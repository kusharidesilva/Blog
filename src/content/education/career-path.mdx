# Choosing the Right Career Path

Making career decisions can feel overwhelming, but a structured approach helps.

## Self-Assessment

### Know Your Interests

- What activities energize you?
- What topics fascinate you?
- What would you do for free?

### Identify Your Skills

- Technical skills
- Soft skills
- Transferable skills

### Understand Your Values

- Work-life balance
- Financial security
- Making an impact
- Creativity and autonomy

## Research Options

### Explore Industries

- Growth potential
- Job availability
- Salary ranges
- Work environment

### Talk to Professionals

- Informational interviews
- LinkedIn connections
- Career fairs

## Gaining Experience

- Internships
- Part-time jobs
- Volunteer work
- Side projects

## Making the Decision

Consider:
- Alignment with values
- Long-term prospects
- Lifestyle fit
- Growth opportunities

Remember: Careers are not linear. It's okay to pivot and explore!
