# Effective Learning Strategies

Learn how to learn more effectively with these proven techniques.

## Active Learning

### Spaced Repetition

- Review material at increasing intervals
- Use flashcard apps like Anki
- Better long-term retention

### Active Recall

- Test yourself regularly
- Don't just re-read—retrieve
- Practice without looking at notes

## Study Techniques

### The Pomodoro Technique

1. Study for 25 minutes
2. Take a 5-minute break
3. After 4 sessions, take a longer break

### Feynman Technique

1. Choose a concept
2. Explain it simply
3. Identify gaps in understanding
4. Review and simplify

## Environment Matters

- Minimize distractions
- Have a dedicated study space
- Good lighting and comfortable seating

## Take Care of Yourself

- Get adequate sleep
- Exercise regularly
- Stay hydrated

Learning is a skill that can be improved with practice!
