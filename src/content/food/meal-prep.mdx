# Easy Meal Prep Ideas

Meal prepping saves time, money, and helps you eat healthier.

## Getting Started

### Essential Equipment

- Quality containers
- Good knives
- Large pots and pans
- Measuring tools

### Planning

1. Choose your recipes
2. Make a shopping list
3. Schedule prep time
4. Start with 3-4 recipes

## Breakfast Ideas

### Overnight Oats

Mix oats, milk, yogurt, and toppings. Ready in the morning!

### Egg Muffins

Bake eggs with vegetables in muffin tins.

### Smoothie Packs

Pre-portion fruits and greens in freezer bags.

## Lunch Ideas

### Grain Bowls

- Base: rice, quinoa, or farro
- Protein: chicken, tofu, or beans
- Vegetables: roasted or raw
- Dressing on the side

### Mason Jar Salads

Layer dressing at bottom, greens on top.

## Dinner Ideas

### Sheet Pan Meals

Roast protein and vegetables together.

### Slow Cooker Dishes

Set it and forget it.

## Storage Tips

- Label everything with dates
- Use within 4-5 days
- Freeze for longer storage

Start small and build your meal prep habit!
