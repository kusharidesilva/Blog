# Baking for Beginners

Baking is both science and art. Master the basics first.

## Essential Equipment

- Measuring cups and spoons
- Mixing bowls
- Baking sheets
- Silicone spatula
- Wire cooling rack
- Stand or hand mixer

## Key Ingredients

### Flour

- All-purpose for most recipes
- Bread flour for yeasted breads
- Cake flour for tender cakes

### Leaveners

- Baking soda: needs acid to activate
- Baking powder: self-activating
- Yeast: for breads

### Fats

- Butter: flavor and texture
- Oil: moisture
- Room temperature matters!

## Baking Tips

### Measure Accurately

- Use proper measuring tools
- Level dry ingredients
- Use a scale for best results

### Follow the Recipe

- Baking is chemistry
- Don't substitute until you understand why

### Temperature Matters

- Preheat your oven
- Room temperature ingredients
- Test for doneness

1. Chocolate chip cookies
2. Banana bread
3. Simple muffins
4. Basic cake

Practice makes perfect—keep baking!
