# World Cuisines to Try

Expand your palate with these delicious cuisines.

## Asian Cuisines

### Japanese

- Sushi and sashimi
- Ramen
- Tempura
- Minimalist, fresh flavors

### Thai

- Pad Thai
- Green curry
- Tom Yum soup
- Balance of sweet, sour, salty, spicy

### Indian

- Curry varieties
- Biryani
- Naan bread
- Complex spice blends

## Mediterranean

### Italian

- Fresh pasta
- Pizza
- Risotto
- Simple, quality ingredients

### Greek

- Mezze platters
- Gyros
- Fresh salads
- Olive oil based

## Latin American

### Mexican

- Tacos
- Mole
- Fresh salsas
- Regional diversity

### Peruvian

- Ceviche
- Lomo saltado
- Fusion flavors

## Middle Eastern

- Hummus
- Falafel
- Shawarma
- Fragrant spices

## Tips for Cooking World Cuisines

- Start with authentic recipes
- Source proper ingredients
- Don't skip the prep
- Taste as you cook

Food is the best way to travel without leaving home!
