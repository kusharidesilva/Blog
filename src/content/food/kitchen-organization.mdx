# Kitchen Organization Tips

An organized kitchen makes cooking more enjoyable.

## Declutter First

### Assess Everything

- Remove items you don't use
- Check expiration dates
- Donate duplicates

### Keep Only What You Need

- Tools you use regularly
- Ingredients you actually cook with
- Quality over quantity

## Storage Solutions

### Pantry Organization

- Use clear containers
- Label everything
- Group similar items
- First in, first out

### Refrigerator

- Clear bins for categories
- Store at proper temperatures
- Clean weekly

### Drawer Dividers

- Separate utensils by type
- Easy access to frequently used items

## Zones

Create kitchen zones:

1. **Prep Zone**: Cutting boards, knives
2. **Cooking Zone**: Pots, pans, utensils
3. **Baking Zone**: Measuring tools, mixers
4. **Storage Zone**: Containers, wraps

## Daily Habits

- Clean as you cook
- Put things back immediately
- Wipe counters daily
- Do dishes promptly

A tidy kitchen is a happy kitchen!
