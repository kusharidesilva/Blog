# Plant-Based Cooking Guide

Discover the joy of cooking with plants.

## Why Plant-Based?

- Health benefits
- Environmental impact
- Animal welfare
- Delicious variety

## Protein Sources

### Legumes

- Lentils
- Chickpeas
- Black beans
- Tofu and tempeh

### Whole Grains

- Quinoa
- Brown rice
- Oats

### Nuts and Seeds

- Almonds
- Chia seeds
- Hemp hearts

## Flavor Boosters

### Umami Sources

- Mushrooms
- Soy sauce
- Nutritional yeast
- Miso paste

### Fresh Herbs

- Basil
- Cilantro
- Parsley

## Easy Recipes to Start

### Buddha Bowls

Grain + protein + vegetables + dressing

### Stir-Fries

Quick, customizable, delicious

### Soups and Stews

Hearty and satisfying

### Smoothie Bowls

## Tips for Success

- Start with familiar dishes
- Experiment with spices
- Plan your meals
- Don't aim for perfection

Plants are delicious—give them a chance!
